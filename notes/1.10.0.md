- **Journeys have phases.** A journey can be divided into labelled sections of its roadmap, such as "Concepting" or "Finishing up", in order, and each step can be put in one. A step with no phase of its own belongs to its objective's.
- **Phases are kept tidy.** Putting a step in a phase the journey does not have is refused with the journey's phases named, and a phase cannot be removed while a step is still in it.
- **/journey proposes phases.** When it designs a journey with you, it suggests an ordered list of phases, puts every step in one, and writes them along with the tree.
