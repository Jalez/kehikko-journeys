- **Search a journey with ⌘F.** Press ⌘F in Journeys to open Kehikot's find bar: every match in what the page shows is highlighted and counted ("3 of 17"), the page scrolls to the current one, Enter and ⇧Enter move between matches, and Escape closes the bar.
- **Your place is kept.** Each Journeys container remembers how far down you had scrolled, and comes back there when you return to the kehikko or reopen the app.
- **No flash while it opens.** Kehikot keeps its cover up until the journey is drawn, so you no longer see a half-drawn page first.
- **A page that fails says so.** If the page cannot be drawn, Kehikot is told what went wrong, and the page offers Reload.
