- **What has to become true for a user, step by step.** A journey lays out an epic as steps, each filed under one part of the epic, with the issues and changes it is about and where each of them stands.
- **Design a journey as a tree.** Use `/journey` in Claude Code to design one as a tree of steps, with which step waits on which and the next step free to start.
- **Gates that block steps.** A step can wait on a decision, on access, or on work that has no issue, and the journey shows what it is waiting for.
- **Pick a step, narrow the canvas.** Ticking a step picks its references out on the canvas, so References and the other modules narrow to what that step is about.
- **Every journey in a project.** With All epics chosen, Journeys lists every epic's journey, read-only.
