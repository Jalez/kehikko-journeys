- **Partial work stands under its issue and says so.** A merge or pull request that says it is part of an issue, rather than closing it, is now drawn under that issue with the changes that close it, and its card says which issue it is part of.
- **One merged part no longer looks like a finished issue.** While only parts of an issue have landed, the issue shows as in progress, and its step stays open until the issue itself is done.
